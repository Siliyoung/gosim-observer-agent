# Experiment E009

## Source

ALTSched (arXiv:1903.00531) and HET queue operations (arXiv:0705.3889).

## Hypothesis

Raising field-change opportunity cost from 300 to 360 seconds will favor exposures that better amortize a pointing and cross completion thresholds.

## Change

Changed `FIELD_CHANGE_COST_SECONDS` from 300 to 360.

## Diagnostics

- robust objective: 4496.231815 -> 4285.581268
- required miss: 8 -> 15 (L4: 2 -> 8)
- invalid action: 0; request miss: 4/8; avoidable wait: unavailable
- other: observe actions fell on all cards

## Analysis

The larger regularizer over-favored long exposures, sharply reducing coverage and required completion.

## Decision

REJECT

## Lesson

The retained 300-second change cost is already near the safe side of the coverage/completion trade-off.
