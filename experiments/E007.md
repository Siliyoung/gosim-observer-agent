## Source

Pandora schedule optimization, arXiv:2305.02285.

## Hypothesis

Increasing request initial value from 50% to 75% will cross the completion threshold or further improve required robustness.

## Analysis

Extra early commitment displaced science on L1/L2 without completing another request or materially improving the worst card.

## Decision

## Lesson

The useful request-pressure range is below 75%; binary rewards alone do not justify aggressive pursuit of infeasible windows.
