# Experiment E012

## Source

Rothchild, Stubbs, and Yoachim (2019), ALTSched: simple deterministic scheduling policies can be competitive, but their switching rules must be evaluated end to end.

## Hypothesis

Requiring a 1.0% relative gain before accepting local fiber refinement will suppress marginal reallocations and improve stability across cards.

## Change

Raised `REFINEMENT_MIN_RELATIVE_GAIN` from 0.005 to 0.010. No other behavior changed.

## Results

| Card | Before | After | Delta |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| L1 | 4728.527875 | 4622.993640 | -105.534235 |
| L2 | 5032.127829 | 3215.027040 | -1817.100789 |
| L3 | 4691.631469 | 4659.897279 | -31.734190 |
| L4 | 4160.179962 | 3993.668986 | -166.510976 |

Robust objective: 4496.231815 → 3829.277756.

## Diagnostics

- required miss: 19 total, including 13 on L2
- invalid action: 0
- avoidable wait: unavailable from the V4 runner
- request miss: 4 of 8
- flexible shortfall: unavailable from the V4 runner

## Analysis

The threshold change caused a discrete trajectory shift rather than merely removing low-value refinements. L2 lost many required observations and collapsed. This is a phase-change parameter, not a harmless efficiency filter.

## Decision

REJECT

## Lesson

Retain the 0.5% threshold. Small local acceptance changes can alter the entire future schedule, so they require full-card evaluation.
