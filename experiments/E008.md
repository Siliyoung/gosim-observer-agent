# Experiment E008

## Source

HET queue scheduling (arXiv:0705.3889) and Pandora window-quality factors (arXiv:2305.02285).

## Hypothesis

Increasing exposure candidates by 15% on nights forecast as overcast, hazy, or cold will improve threshold completion under poor quality.

## Change

Set deterministic `duration_scale=1.15` for those public forecast kinds.

## Results

| Card | Before | After | Delta |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| L1 | 4728.527875 | 4602.392849 | -126.135026 |
| L2 | 5032.127829 | 4898.964899 | -133.162930 |
| L3 | 4691.631469 | 4671.150445 | -20.481024 |
| L4 | 4160.179962 | 4137.028160 | -23.151802 |

## Diagnostics

- robust objective: 4496.231815 -> 4438.922648
- required miss: 8 -> 10; invalid action: 0; request miss: 4/8
- avoidable wait: unavailable; all four cards regressed

## Analysis

Forecast categories were too coarse to prescribe exposure length. Uniformly longer exposures reduced throughput and increased required misses.

## Decision

REJECT

## Lesson

Forecasts work better as directional filters; exposure duration should remain tied to current learned quality and score/time.
