# Experiment E013

## Source

Shetrone et al. (2007), queue scheduling for the Hobby-Eberly Telescope, emphasizing matching observations to forecast conditions.

## Hypothesis

Treating haze as usable rather than avoiding its forecast direction will recover observing opportunities without increasing required misses.

## Change

Removed `haze` from the deterministic adverse-forecast direction set. All other weather handling remained unchanged.

## Analysis

Although haze is not a hard closure, the public forecast still carries useful directional opportunity-cost information. Ignoring it worsened three cards and the worst-card score.

## Decision

REJECT

## Lesson

Keep haze in the adverse-forecast direction set. Soft weather signals can be valuable without being modeled as mandatory downtime.
