## Hypothesis

Removing the card-ID gate and applying the same bounded local field refinement whenever the generic computation pace is not degraded will preserve L1-L3 and improve or preserve L4.

## Change

Changed the local-refinement condition from an explicit `D`/`L4` exclusion to `fast_level == 0` for every card.

## Analysis

The same refinement produced an identical trajectory on L1-L3 but changed L4's long-horizon trajectory for the worse. A locally larger approximate field value does not necessarily cover its downstream opportunity cost under heavier disruptions and data invalidation.

## Decision

REJECT

## Lesson

Card-specific gating is not acceptable, but unconditional refinement is not a safe replacement. A general trigger must reject small myopic gains using only observable state.
