# Experiment E004

## Source

Pandora scheduling (arXiv:2305.02285) and rolling-horizon deadline protection.

## Hypothesis

Restoring the full required completion value in the final two visible nights, rather than only the final night, will leave one recovery opportunity and reduce misses.

## Change

Changed required terminal-value restoration from `last_night <= current_night` to `last_night <= current_night + 1` and updated its unit test.
